# Third-party assets

Fonts, icons, images, data and palettes that live in the repository or are included as data. Rule: any new asset is added only with a license that permits redistribution and with a line in this table; if the origin is unclear, don't add it. Licenses were checked against the license files, `package.json` and the READMEs of the original sources; "to be clarified" means that the origin or the rights are not confirmed.

Library dependencies (Cargo, npm) are not listed here, except those that ship data or icons: their licenses are in `Cargo.lock`, `web/package-lock.json` and in the packages themselves.
