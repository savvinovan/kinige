## Scenario
1. I open Inbox. Above the feed there is a thin strip: this year as a 7 × 53 grid (weekdays × weeks), cells tinted by season and darker for fuller days, month letters on top. The day at the top of the feed is outlined.
2. I press the strip's expand button. The strip grows into the isometric forest of the year; the feed stays below it. Winter at both ends, spring, summer, autumn between; empty days are bare ground. "‹ 2026 ›" switches years (from the year of my oldest entry to this year); "Whole year" and "Winter / Spring / Summer / Autumn" zoom to that part of the board without filtering anything.
3. I hover a tree. A card: "Fri, October 16, 2026 · 5 notes · a large tree" and the first line of that day's first entry. I click it: the feed below opens on that day's first entry with the same highlight as a jump from search. Double click zooms in ×2; wheel and pinch zoom, dragging pans.
4. I scroll the feed up into September. The outlined cell follows the day at the top of the feed; when the feed crosses into 2025, the board and the strip switch to 2025.
5. With the keyboard: Tab into the board, ←/→ previous/next day, ↑/↓ previous/next week, Enter opens the day in the feed, Escape clears the selection. VoiceOver reads "October 16, 5 notes, a large tree".
6. I switch "Forest / Calendar": twelve ordinary month grids with the same data (a dot sized by stage); clicking a day does the same as clicking a tree.
7. I write a note today. If today's stage goes up, today's tree grows once (700 ms; it jumps under Reduce Motion). Nothing else on the board ever moves by itself. I delete the note: the tree is smaller, without animation.
8. I press the strip's "Hide forest" button: the strip is gone and the feed takes its place. Profile → "Show forest" brings the strip back.
9. `kill -9`, launch: the forest is in the same state (hidden, strip or expanded; Forest or Calendar).

## Included
- **Data (Rust).** A day counts live messages (`deleted_at IS NULL`) of chats not in the trash, all chats including archived ones (user decision 2). `notes` = count; weight and stage by `day_weight` / `stage_of` (`attachments = 0` until slice 7; `DEFAULT_W_MAX` stays a code constant). `excerpt` = `reply::excerpt` of the day's first entry by `(journal_date, created_at, id)`. One `kinige-store` query per year, a range over the `message_day` index; no `day_activity` cache table and no migration. The per-day sums are done by SQLite (one `GROUP BY journal_date`, about 365 rows reach Rust). The budget (`get_forest_year` p95 < 100 ms on the bench) is missed on the CI macOS VM (p95 244 ms; local release build 83 ms): the user decided on 03.11.2026 to keep the query, check it on the real Mac at acceptance, and add a derived per-day stats table only later in KINIG-47 if it is noticeable. A pure `forest::year_table(year, first_weekday, stats)` in `kinige-domain` builds the table.
- **How the frontend gets the rules (ADR-0013 left it to this slice):** Rust returns the whole year table (every day: date, row, col, season, notes, stage, excerpt) plus `cols`, `min_year`, `today`. TypeScript keeps only geometry (iso transform, hit-test point → cell, cell → day by lookup). No wasm build, no TS copy of the calendar rules. First weekday: `Intl.Locale(navigator.language).getWeekInfo()`, then `weekInfo`, then Monday (ADR-0013), in one small function; passed as an argument. At start the main window logs at `debug` which source gave the value (`getWeekInfo`, `weekInfo`, `fallback`) and the result (`monday`/`sunday`): no locale string, no user data.
- **IPC (§12.3), `main` only:** `get_forest_year(year, first_weekday)` → `ForestYear`; `get_forest_day_target(date, current_chat_id)` → `{chat_id, message_id}` or `null` (the open chat if it has entries that day, otherwise the chat with the most entries that day, Inbox on a tie). The jump goes through the existing `FocusTarget` + `list_messages(around)` path (`useFeed`, as search and slice 5). Refresh on the existing "entries changed" event. `capture` keeps exactly seven commands; `settings` is denied both. ts-rs types in `web/src/api/generated/`.
- **View state:** `app_setting.forest_view = hidden | strip | expanded` (default `strip`; user decision 1), `app_setting.forest_mode = forest | calendar`; "Hide forest" is an `IconButton` on the strip and the expanded header, "Show forest" / "Hide forest" is an item of the profile menu (the only way back, there is no sidebar item); both local, not synced, not in `settings.json` (like `sidebar_collapsed`).
- **Renderer** (port of the spike, `web/src/ui/forest/`): Canvas 2D with the layer cache (re-render on data/selection change, 140 ms after the camera rests, when the view leaves the layer); three levels of detail by tile width (dot < 14 px, simplified < 32 px, detailed ≥ 32 px) with **placeholder shapes**; month labels on the front edge and weekday letters on the left edge, thinned on small tiles; selected cell outlined, trees in front of it semi-transparent; future days and cells around the year are ground without interaction; the canvas is one focusable element (label "Forest of 2026") and the selected day is announced in a live region. Shape geometry lives in one constants file (`forest/shapes.ts`) that slice 9 replaces with atlases; every colour (season ground and canopy, outline, labels) is a token `color.forest.*` in `design/tokens/` read from CSS variables. Season zoom: Winter → January–February end.
- **Collapsed strip:** a flat 7 × 53/54 heat map like GitHub's (user decision 3), DOM `role="grid"` with a roving tabindex (↑/↓ day, ←/→ week, matching its layout), the same card on hover and selection, click = jump. No zoom or pan.
- **Calendar alternative:** DOM month grids, first weekday as above, each day a button with the screen reader label; arrows as in the board.
- **Day card** (design-system component, tokens only, `surface.card`): date, "N notes" with ru/en plurals, stage name (7 strings: bare ground, a sprout, a seedling, a sapling, a young tree, a tree, a large tree), first line up to two lines with an ellipsis; no photos line until slice 7 (§7.4 "only what exists"). Shown on hover and for the keyboard/feed selection when nothing is hovered.
- **Growth animation:** only when today's stage rises while the board is visible; duration and curve from `motion.*` tokens; `prefers-reduced-motion` jumps. 0 frames while idle.
- **Documents:** `CLAUDE.md` in the first commit, "State": "slice D2 visual refresh accepted 02.11.2026; slice 10a Windows build accepted 02.11.2026". PRD v0.17: §7.4 (placement and strip as decided), §12.3 (matrix), §15 row 6 ("simple assets, one level of detail + dot" → "placeholder shapes, three levels of detail", user decision 5), history. ADR-0013: a "Slice 6" paragraph (Rust year table, no wasm).

## Checks
- **Rust domain:** spike tests of `forest.rs` stay; `year_table`: every day present once, 54-column years, short notes counted in `notes` but not in weight, seasons per date.
- **Rust store:** trash (message and chat) excluded, archived chat included, year boundaries (31.12 / 01.01), first-entry excerpt across chats, day-target rule (open chat, most entries, Inbox tie, empty day → `null`); garbage: year 0, −1, `i32::MAX`, unknown weekday, bad dates (panic hunter, `ipc_slice6.rs`). Bench on `import_bench synthetic 100000`: `get_forest_year` p95 < 100 ms, opening Inbox still < 300 ms, cold start < 1.5 s (the strip loads after the first feed page) → `bench.txt`, `cold-start.txt`. `log_privacy`: no dates, excerpts or chat names in the log.
- **IPC (mock):** allowed for `main`, denied for `capture` and `settings`; capture still has seven commands.
- **First weekday (not verified in S3 on the real engines):** vitest of the fallback chain with a stubbed `Intl.Locale`: `getWeekInfo()` with `firstDay` 1 → Monday and 7 → Sunday; only the `weekInfo` property; neither → Monday; `getWeekInfo` throws, or `firstDay` is 0, 8, `NaN`, a string → Monday. Real-engine probe: the runner scenarios read the `debug` line (source and result) from the app log into `week-info.txt` on macOS (WKWebView) and Windows (WebView2), with the runner's locale noted; a missing `getWeekInfo` is a finding, not a failure, as long as the fallback is used.
- **Frontend on `MockApi`:** hit-test (point → cell, `NaN` → none, every cell centre under several cameras), keyboard in board/strip/calendar, click → chat switch + `FocusTarget`, selection follows the feed's top day and year, growth only on stage rise and not under reduced motion, view state persisted, labels, ru/en keys and plurals. Visual baselines via `visual-update.yml`; in the `visual` job a pointer click-through of every day of 2026 (Monday) and 2028 (Sunday) on the expanded story, 0 misses.
- **Screenshots `docs/screens/6/`** (`scripts/screens/6.sh`, macOS runner, AX input; `seed_demo` gets a synthetic full year with every stage, a week-long gap, several chats on some days, a trashed entry): 01 Inbox with the strip; 02 expanded whole year; 03 autumn zoom; 05 feed below the strip; 06 feed moved to an older day, selection followed; 07 previous year; 08 calendar; 08b a click on a calendar day opens the day in the feed; 09–10 today's tree before/after a sent note; 11 en; 12 large text; 13 640 px window; 14 forest hidden (via the strip button); 15 after `kill -9` (still hidden), 16 shown again from the profile menu. Only the shots with new UI text (01, 02, 08b, 11, 14, 16) are dumped in full (the window tree takes minutes on the runner); every dumped shot is checked for completeness and personal paths. The caption of the selected day must be in the accessibility tree (checked on the dump of 02). Texts: `bench.txt`, `cold-start.txt`, `week-info.txt`. **Windows** (`scripts/screens/6.ps1`, `-f os=windows`): 01 strip, 02 expanded, 03 calendar; `week-info.txt`.
- **Click to a day, evidence:** an accessibility press on the canvas has no point to land on, so the macOS scenario cannot click a cell of the board. The evidence is (1) the real-window click on a calendar day (08b), (2) the Playwright click-through with a real pointer on every day of 2026 (365) and 2028 (366, Sunday first, the 54th column included) with 0 misses, (3) the keyboard path (arrows, Enter) and the click-to-feed chain in vitest on `MockApi`.
- **Real hardware:** the user pans and zooms on the real Mac at acceptance and judges the smoothness by hand; no FPS number reaches the log (a window has no channel to it, and adding one would widen the window's permissions). A dev build (`scripts/run-app.sh`, Web Inspector) prints each finished gesture's frame rate to the console.

4. **Southern Hemisphere:** not in slice 6 (`season_of_month` already takes `southern`).
