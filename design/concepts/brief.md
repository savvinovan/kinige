# Brief for Codex: three feed variants (slice D1)

Status: **approved by the user on 20.10.2026**. The concepts were generated by Codex (`$imagegen`): `design/concepts/feed/`.

Sources: PRD §11.2 (direction), §6 (feed), §1.2 items 2–3 (typography, content matters more than interface); `docs/slices/D1-design-foundation.md`. The PRD names the variants (Editorial / Conversational / Immersive) but does not describe them. The description below is my interpretation; the user approves it too.

## What these images are

- **A mood reference, not a specification.** Pixels and the exact font are not copied: tokens and components are written in the repository (§11.1).
- The goal: pick one of the three feed variants. Only the chosen one is implemented.
- The model draws letters with mistakes, especially Cyrillic. This is expected; we look at rhythm, margins, hierarchy and color, not spelling. The entry texts are given exactly below and must be used verbatim.

## Common to all three

- A macOS window, 1280 × 800, in the dark "dark paper" theme: a warm dark background (almost black with a brown undertone), soft light ivory text, one muted ochre accent, thin dividers. The palette is the same on all six images, so that we compare the variants, not the colors.
- The color on the concepts (ochre, warm background) is not a palette choice: the user picks the palette separately in Storybook, out of three candidates.
- **Forbidden:** purple and blue neon, gradients, glow, glass, message bubbles, avatars, shadowed cards around entries, emoji, level badges, the pet, the forest, illustrations. The forest, the pet and "On this day" are not part of D1.
- Entry text is a book serif with Cyrillic (like Literata or Source Serif); time, captions and the interface are a neutral sans-serif (like Inter); code is monospaced. No system handwritten or decorative fonts.
- The screen structure comes from the mockup `docs/plan/references/mockup-forest-desktop.png` (structure only, not style): a sidebar on the left (placeholder), the feed in the center, the input field at the bottom. Sidebar: "Поиск" (Search), "Входящие" (Inbox) (selected), a list of 4 chats: Идеи (Ideas), Книги (Books), Работа (Work), Рецепты (Recipes). Window title: "Входящие" (Inbox).
- The input field is a plain field, not a panel with buttons: the placeholder "Написать заметку…" (Write a note…) and, small on the right, "Enter — отправить, Shift+Enter — новая строка" (Enter to send, Shift+Enter for a new line).
- Time uses the 24-hour format, `09:12`. Day dividers: "Сегодня" (Today), "Вчера" (Yesterday), and a date with the year for past years: "14 марта 2025" (14 March 2025).
- Adjacent entries less than 5 minutes apart merge into a "page": only the first one shows its time.

## How the variants differ

The difference is in **rhythm and in how much interface remains around the text**, not in color.

### 1. Editorial

The feed as a typeset magazine column.

- One centered column with a narrow measure (about 65 characters), wide margins.
- The day divider is a heading: a large serif with a thin rule below it. The heading text follows the common rule ("Сегодня" (Today), "Вчера" (Yesterday), "14 марта 2025" (14 March 2025)). A feature of this variant: below the heading, small, in the secondary color, the weekday and the date in words ("вторник, 20 октября" (Tuesday, 20 October)), as in magazine layout; this does not affect the heading rule.
- Time is small in the left margin (in the column margin), in the secondary color, not on the line with the text.
- Entries have no borders and no noticeable gaps: the paragraphs of one "page" follow one another, with only paragraph spacing between entries.
- The composer is a thin rule and a field, with no fill.

### 2. Conversational

The familiar rhythm of a messenger, but without bubbles.

- A wider column (about 80 characters), aligned to the left edge of the feed.
- The day divider is a compact centered label ("Сегодня" (Today)) with a rule on the left and on the right.
- Time is on a separate small line right above the text of each entry, in the secondary color (the chat name "Входящие" (Inbox) is not written next to the time); merged entries have the time hidden.
- Each entry is separated from its neighbor by a noticeable gap and a very thin rule; you can see where one entry ends and the next begins.
- The composer is a field with a light fill and rounded corners at the bottom, as in a messenger.

### 3. Immersive

Maximum text, minimum interface: like reading a book in silence.

- The sidebar is collapsed into a narrow strip of icons or hidden entirely; there is no window title.
- The text is noticeably larger (by eye +15% over Editorial), the margins are very wide, the leading is loose.
- The day divider is a quiet caption in small caps, with no rules and a large top margin; the text follows the common rule ("Сегодня" (Today), "Вчера" (Yesterday), "14 марта 2025" (14 March 2025)), with no weekday.
- Time appears only on the first entry of the day and is very pale; the rest appears on hover.
- The composer is a single line at the bottom with no border and no fill, the placeholder barely visible; while typing only the cursor remains.

## How we check the result

The user answers these questions: which rhythm is more pleasant to reread? Does the interface weigh on the text? Is it comfortable for the eye on long entries? Can you see without captions where a day and an entry end? The choice is recorded in an ADR; the concepts stay in the repository as a mood reference.
